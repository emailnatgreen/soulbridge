## XRPL Ecosystem Alignment

SoulBridge directly contributes to XRPL ecosystem growth through:

1. **On-chain transactions**: Every agent action generates XRPL transactions (DID, payments, NFTs, trustlines)
2. **RLUSD adoption**: Primary currency driving real utility for Ripple's stablecoin
3. **Developer tooling**: Open patterns for DID, multi-sig, and NFT integration
4. **Institutional bridge**: UK FSMA 2026 compliance creates a regulated pathway for institutional adoption
5. **Innovation showcase**: First constitutionally governed AI economy on any blockchain
